rollout-stats

Is your policy's improvement real?

Exact statistics for robot evaluations with 10–100 rollouts. Everything runs in your browser.

How sure is one success rate?

95% Wilson interval — well behaved even at 0/20 or 20/20.

Is A better than B?

Two-sided Fisher exact test and Newcombe interval for the difference (independent rollouts).

How many rollouts do I need?

Exact power of Fisher's test, by enumerating every possible outcome.

Python package, paired tests, multiple-comparison correction and anytime-valid sequential tests: github.com/WayneUSC/rollout-stats
